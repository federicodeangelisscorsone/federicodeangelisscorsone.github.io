Federico De Angelis Scorsone

home | bio | cv | education | experiences | writing

Federico De Angelis Scorsone is a PhD student in the double doctoral program in Finance between Sapienza University (Rome, Italy) and Southwestern University of Finance and Economics (Chengdu, China), since November 2024.

He received his BSc degree in Economics from Roma Tre University in 2021 and his MSc in Economics cum laude from the same university in 2024. He is a MathWorks Certified MATLAB Associate, holds an IELTS score of 8.00/9.00 and passed the HSK 2 Chinese proficiency test.

Mr. De Angelis Scorsone served on various institutional bodies, such as the Board of Directors and the Academic Senate of Roma Tre University, as well as the Youth Council of Rome’s II District.

In addition, he has been involved in various volunteering experiences. He spent 3 months in 2018 volunteering with AIESEC International in Azerbaijan, and he is currently a member of Rotaract, a Rotary-sponsored association.

He was born in Rome on July 29, 1998

Notable achievements

  • In 2025, the China State Council awarded Mr. De Angelis Scorsone the Young Envoys Scholarship (YES program).
  • In 2024, the Italy-USA Foundation awarded him the 'America Youth Award for College Talent' and a scholarship for the Executive Master in Leadership for International Relations and Made in Italy.
  • In 2018, he received the Young Entrepreneurs Scholarship from a private company.
  • As a student representative at Roma Tre University, he was one of the few students who served in almost all central bodies of academic governance.
